## Purpose

This document outlines Nuri's short-term, medium-term, and long-term business objectives. It serves as a strategic planning framework to guide decision-making, resource allocation, and performance measurement across all departments.

## Overview

## Goal-Setting Methodology

### SMART Framework
All goals follow SMART principles:
- **Specific**: Clear and well-defined
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable metrics established
- **Achievable**: Based on resources and market conditions
- **Relevant**: Aligned with company strategy
- **Time-bound**: Specific deadlines set

### Alignment Process
1. **Quarterly Review**: Align goals with market conditions
2. **Monthly Check-ins**: Track progress and adjust as needed
3. **Annual Strategy Session**: Comprehensive goal reassessment
4. **Budget Integration**: Ensure resource availability
5. **Team Involvement**: Gather input from all departments

## Review and Adjustment Process

### Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
- **Date**: End of each quarter
- **Participants**: Founder, key stakeholders
- **Focus**: Goal progress, challenges, upcoming priorities
- **Outcomes**: Goal adjustments, resource reallocation

### Monthly Check-ins
- **Frequency**: First week of each month
- **Participants**: Core team members
- **Focus**: Weekly progress, immediate issues
- **Outcomes**: Tactical adjustments, support needs

### Annual Strategic Planning
- **Frequency**: Mid-year review and year-end planning
- **Participants**: All stakeholders
- **Focus**: Long-term direction, major goal adjustments
- **Outcomes**: Strategic realignment, resource planning

## Purpose

This roadmap outlines Nuri's strategic growth trajectory, key milestones, and phase-based development plan. It serves as a living document to track progress and align resources with business objectives.

## Overview

Nuri operates on a 6-phase growth framework, with each phase building upon previous achievements and setting the stage for future expansion. The roadmap balances organic growth with strategic initiatives while maintaining the company's core values and founder vision.

## Phase 1: Website Launch

### Timeline
July 2026 - August 2026

### Objectives
1. **Complete product catalog** with high-quality images and detailed descriptions
2. **Launch functional e-commerce website** with secure payment processing
3. **Establish customer service infrastructure**
4. **Build initial social media presence**
5. **Create brand guidelines and assets**

### Key Deliverables
- Fully responsive website
- Product photography studio setup
- Payment gateway integration (Razorpay)
- Initial marketing content
- Customer support procedures

### Success Metrics
- Website uptime > 99%
- Page load time < 3 seconds
- Customer satisfaction score > 4.5/5

## Risk Management

### High-Impact Risks
- **Supply chain disruptions**: Maintain 3-month inventory buffer
- **Competitive pressure**: Focus on unique Korean cultural positioning
- **Regulatory changes**: Stay updated on international trade regulations
- **Economic downturns**: Maintain flexible cost structure

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **Diversified supplier base** across multiple regions
2. **Strong brand differentiation** through cultural storytelling
3. **Continuous legal and regulatory monitoring**
4. **Lean operational model** with scalable systems

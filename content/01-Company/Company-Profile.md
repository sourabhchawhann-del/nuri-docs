### Business Description

Nuri is a Korean-inspired lifestyle e-commerce brand specializing in high-quality stickers, stationery, and cute accessories. The brand aims to bring "small things, quiet joy" to everyday life through carefully curated products that combine Korean aesthetic sensibilities with practical functionality.

### Products

1. **Korean Stickers** — High-quality vinyl stickers featuring Korean designs, patterns, and cultural elements.
2. **Korean Stationery** — Premium notebooks, planners, and paper goods with Korean design elements.
3. **Cute Accessories** — Keychains, charms, and other lifestyle accessories.
4. **Future Korean Lifestyle Products** — Planned expansion into additional Korean-inspired lifestyle categories.

### Target Audience

- **Age 16–35** Korean culture enthusiasts, students, and design-conscious buyers.
- **Tech-savvy digital natives** who appreciate quality design.
- **Collectors and enthusiasts** of Korean pop culture and stationery.
- **Gift buyers** looking for unique, culturally-inspired presents.

### Business Model

- **Direct-to-Consumer E-commerce** — Online-only retail with shipping across India and select international locations.
- **Custom merchandise** services for individuals and brands (future).
- **Strategic partnerships** with creators and cultural platforms (future).

#### Revenue Streams
- Product sales (single items and bundles).
- Custom design services (planned).
- Affiliate and collaboration revenue (planned).

# Nuri — Company Profile

## About Nuri

Nuri is a Korean-inspired lifestyle e-commerce brand specializing in high-quality stickers, stationery, and cute accessories. We bring "small things, quiet joy" into everyday life through carefully curated products that combine Korean aesthetic sensibilities with practical functionality.

## Legal Structure

Nuri is operated as an unincorporated sole proprietorship by its founder, Shubham Chawhan.

## Our Products

- **Korean Stickers** — High-quality vinyl stickers featuring Korean designs, patterns, and cultural elements.
- **Korean Stationery** — Premium notebooks, planners, and paper goods with Korean design elements.
- **Cute Accessories** — Keychains, charms, and other lifestyle accessories.
- **Future Korean Lifestyle Products** — We're always expanding into new Korean-inspired lifestyle categories.

## Our Audience

- **Age 16–35** — Korean culture enthusiasts, students, and design-conscious buyers.
- **Tech-savvy digital natives** who appreciate quality design.
- **Collectors and enthusiasts** of Korean pop culture and stationery.
- **Gift buyers** looking for unique, culturally-inspired presents.

## How We Work

- **Direct-to-Consumer E-commerce** — Online-only retail with shipping across India and select international locations.
- **Custom merchandise** services for individuals and brands.
- **Strategic partnerships** with creators and cultural platforms.

### Revenue Streams

- Product sales (single items and bundles).
- Custom design services.
- Affiliate and collaboration revenue.

## Mission

To bring small things, quiet joy into everyday life through authentic Korean-inspired design, creating meaningful connections between people and their personal spaces.

## Vision

To become a trusted source for Korean-inspired lifestyle products, recognized for our quality, cultural authenticity, and commitment to bringing beauty into daily routines.

## Long-Term Goals

1. **Year 1–2** — Establish a strong market presence in India.
2. **Year 3–5** — Expand product lines and explore international shipping.
3. **Year 5+** — Grow into a recognized Korean lifestyle brand.
4. **Throughout** — Maintain deliberate, sustainable growth.

# Email Marketing Plan

- **Document ID**: MK-008
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Created Date**: July 19, 2026
- **Status**: Draft

## Principles
- Permission-based only (opt-in).
- One clear purpose per email.
- Honor unsubscribe immediately.

## Email Types
1. **Welcome** — thank-you + brand story + first-order code.
2. **New Drop** — announce new products with photography.
3. **Restock** — notify interest lists.
4. **Promotion** — seasonal/limited discounts.
5. **Care/Community** — UGC features, tips.

## Cadence
- Pre-launch: weekly waitlist updates.
- Post-launch: 1–2 emails/week max.

## Tools
- Start with a free/low-cost provider; integrate via backend email service.

## Compliance
- Include physical/contact identifier and unsubscribe link.
- Respect cookie/consent preferences for marketing tracking.

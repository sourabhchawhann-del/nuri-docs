# Nuri Launch Strategy

- **Document ID**: MK-002
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Created Date**: July 19, 2026
- **Status**: Draft

## Purpose

Defines the go-to-market plan for Nuri's first customers.

## Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Now)
- Finalize product photography and descriptions.
- Build email waitlist via Instagram bio and website.
- Soft-test checkout with founder test orders.

## Phase 1 — Soft Launch
- Announce to waitlist and close followers.
- Limited "first 100 orders" thank-you note.
- Collect feedback and fix friction.

## Phase 2 — Public Launch
- Instagram Reels campaign + Pinterest pins.
- Launch discount code for first week.
- Encourage UGC with a branded hashtag.

## Launch Checklist
- [ ] Product catalog complete
- [ ] Policies published (refund, shipping, privacy, terms)
- [ ] Cookie consent active
- [ ] Email capture live
- [ ] Payment (Razorpay) tested
- [ ] Analytics consent wired

## Success Criteria
- First 25 paid orders within 30 days of public launch.

## Purpose

This document defines Nuri's marketing strategy for the pre-launch and early-growth phases, focused on
organic, low-cost channels suited to a bootstrap startup.

## Positioning

"Small things, quiet joy." A Korean-inspired lifestyle brand offering cute, design-led stickers,
stationery, and accessories.

## Target Audience

- Age 16–35, Korean-culture and stationery enthusiasts.
- Students, gift buyers, and collectors.
- Active on Instagram, Pinterest, and YouTube.

## Channels (Priority Order)

1. **Instagram** — primary visual channel (Reels, Stories, product posts).
2. **Pinterest** — discovery for stationery and decor inspiration.
3. **Email** — owned audience, promotions and launches.
4. **Community** — replies, UGC, micro-influencers.
5. **SEO** — long-tail content around Korean stickers and stationery.

## Budget Principle

Spend only what can be measured. Prefer organic and partnership-based growth over paid ads until
revenue supports it.

## Metrics

- Follower growth, engagement rate, email subscribers.
- Conversion rate from social to store.
- Customer acquisition cost (once paid channels begin).

## Future Revision Notes

Update quarterly as channels and budget evolve.

## Visual Style
- Bright, airy, nature-toned backgrounds (greens, cream, wood).
- Consistent filters: slight warmth, soft contrast.
- Product fills 60–70% of frame; context fills the rest.

## Caption Formula
- Hook (1 line) → Value (2–3 lines) → CTA (shop / save / comment) → Hashtags.

## Hashtag Sets (rotate)
- Brand: #Nuri #NuriStudio
- Category: #KoreanStickers #KoreanStationery #CuteStationery #DeskSetup
- Community: #StationeryAddict #KoreanAesthetic #SmallBusinessIndia

## Reels Ideas
- "Pack an order with me"
- "New sticker drop"
- "Desk reset routine"
- "How this sticker is made"

## Rules
- No misleading claims about products.
- Credit any collaborator or artist.
- Keep links in bio, not captions.

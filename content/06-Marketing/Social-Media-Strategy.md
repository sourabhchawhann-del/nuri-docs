## Platforms
- **Instagram** (primary): Reels 3–4/week, Stories daily, posts 3–5/week.
- **Pinterest** (discovery): 10–15 pins/week from product and blog content.
- **YouTube** (planned): unboxing and "desk setup" shorts.

## Content Pillars
1. Product showcases (new drops, close-ups).
2. Behind the scenes (packing, design process).
3. Lifestyle (desk setups, journaling).
4. Community (reposts, Q&A, polls).

## Posting Cadence
- Batch-create content weekly.
- Post at audience-active hours (evenings IST).
- Use a content calendar (see Promotion-Calendar.md).

## Voice
Friendly, warm, minimal. Emojis used sparingly. Avoid hard-sell language.

# Nuri Company Documentation System

> Internal corporate records for **Nuri** — a Korean-inspired lifestyle e-commerce brand (stickers,
> stationery, cute accessories). Operated by sole founder **Shubham Chawhan** (100% ownership),
> headquartered in **Vadodara, Gujarat, India**. Currently an **unincorporated sole proprietorship**
> in the pre-launch stage.

These documents are internal business records. They do **not** provide legal advice and do **not**
imply that Nuri is incorporated. Any future incorporation or ownership change will be recorded through
formal written updates.

---

## How to Navigate

Each folder below contains Markdown (`.md`) documents and, where relevant, printable HTML versions.
Legal pages referenced (e.g. `/refund`, `/cookie-policy`) are live routes in the Next.js storefront.

## Live Website Legal Pages

Reachable from the site footer (path → document intent):
`/privacy` · `/terms` · `/refund` · `/shipping` · `/cookie-policy` · `/cookie-preferences` ·
`/disclaimer` · `/copyright` · `/ip-notice` · `/acceptable-use` · `/accessibility` · `/dmca` ·
`/consumer-rights` · `/legal-contact`

A cookie consent banner (Accept / Deny / Customise) stores choice in `localStorage` and links to
`/cookie-preferences`.

## Maintenance

- Update the related Markdown and the live page together when a policy changes.
- Bump the version and "Last Updated" date on every edit.
- Move superseded files into `10-Archive/`.

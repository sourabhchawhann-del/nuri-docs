# Nuri Brand Book

## Version 1.0 - July 2026

## Purpose

This Brand Book serves as the definitive guide for all visual, verbal, and experiential aspects of the Nuri brand. It ensures consistency across all touchpoints and provides clear guidelines for anyone creating content or materials on behalf of Nuri.

## Brand Identity Overview

### Brand Name: Nuri

Nuri is a Korean-inspired lifestyle brand that embraces the philosophy of "small things, quiet joy." The name reflects Korean cultural elements and the brand's mission to bring subtle, meaningful moments to everyday life through carefully curated products.

### Brand Promise

**"Small things, quiet joy."**

Nuri believes that beauty and happiness exist in the little details of life - in a perfect Korean design, a thoughtful stationery item, or a beautifully crafted accessory. Our products are designed to bring these quiet moments of joy into daily routines.

## Brand Personality

### Human: Approachable, Welcoming, Intuitive
Nuri feels like a trusted friend who understands your aesthetic needs and lifestyle preferences. The brand is approachable and intuitive, making even sophisticated Korean design elements feel familiar and accessible.

### Innovative: Forward-thinking, Creative, Trend-setting
While rooted in Korean traditions, Nuri pushes creative boundaries and sets trends in lifestyle design. The brand stays ahead of the curve while remaining grounded in cultural authenticity.

### Refined: Discerning, Elegant, Sophisticated
There's a certain elegance and sophistication to Nuri products that appeals to those with refined taste. The brand maintains high standards for design, quality, and presentation.

### Nurturing: Caring, Supportive, Warm
Nuri creates experiences that nurture emotional well-being. The brand is warm and supportive, bringing joy and comfort through thoughtful product design and brand storytelling.

## Brand Voice

### Communication Style:
- **Warm and conversational** - Like talking to a friend who shares your aesthetic values
- **Culturally informed** - References Korean culture with respect and authenticity
- **Thoughtful and intentional** - Every word and design choice serves a purpose
- **Refined yet accessible** - High-end feel without being pretentious
- **Inspirational but grounded** - Dreams with practical applications

### Examples:

#### Good:
"Discover how a beautifully designed sticker can transform your workspace into a personal sanctuary. Nuri brings Korean-inspired aesthetics to everyday moments, creating spaces where small details matter."

#### Better:
"Transform your workspace with Korean-inspired design. From subtle patterns that tell stories to functional accessories that enhance your day. Nuri believes small details create quiet joy."

### Writing Rules:
1. **Use "Nuri" not "the brand Nuri" or "this Nuri brand"**
2. **Korean cultural elements should be specific and authentic**
3. **Product descriptions focus on emotional benefit, not just features**
4. **Use "K-" prefix when appropriate (K-style, K-aesthetic)**
5. **Reference Korean concepts when culturally relevant (like cooling, harmony, etc.)**

## Visual Identity

### Logo System

#### Primary Logo:
- **Name**: Nuri
- **Typeface**: Custom refined serif with subtle organic curves
- **Color**: Primary wooden/teal palette
- **Shape**: Balanced circular and rectangular elements suggesting harmony

#### Logo Variations:
1. **Full Logo** (text + symbol) - Used for primary brand presentations
2. **Symbol Only** - Used for social media avatars, app icons
3. **Text Only** - Used when visual symbol is impractical
4. **Reversed Logo** - White text on colored background for contrast
5. **Icono-text** - Abstract interpretation of the symbol incorporated into typography

### Typography

#### Primary Font (Headings, Titles):
- **Family**: "Playfair Display" - A serif font that combines elegance with readability
- **Weight**: 400 (Light), 700 (Bold)
- **Style**: Regular Italic for emphasis, Bold Italic for strong statements
- **Alternative**: ForHeaders (web-safe serif alternative)

#### Secondary Font (Body, Descriptions):
- **Family**: "Source Sans Pro" - Clean, modern, highly readable sans-serif
- **Weight**: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-Bold)
- **Style**: All styles available for flexibility
- **Alternative**: Inter (modern variable font alternative)

#### Typography Scale:
- **H1 (Homepage Hero)**: 72px - 56px (responsive) - Bold Playfair Display
- **H2 (Section Titles)**: 48px - 36px (responsive) - Bold Playfair Display
- **H3 (Subsections)**: 36px - 28px (responsive) - Semi-Bold Source Sans Pro
- **Body Large**: 20px - 18px (responsive) - Regular Source Sans Pro
- **Body Regular**: 16px - 15px (responsive) - Regular Source Sans Pro
- **Body Small**: 14px - 13px (responsive) - Light Source Sans Pro
- **Caption**: 12px - 11px (responsive) - Light Source Sans Pro

### Logo Usage Rules

#### Clear Space:
- **Minimum**: The logo should have a clear space equivalent to half its height on all sides
- **No text or graphics should overlap the logo symbol**
- **Maintain proportions**: Do not stretch or compress the logo

#### Minimum Size:
- **Print**: 1.5cm (0.6 inches) minimum width
- **Digital**: 16px width (equivalent) on standard displays
- **Icons**: Minimum 24px × 24px for application icons

#### Requirements:
1. **Do not modify the logo colors or fonts**
2. **Do not create derivative logos**
3. **Do not rotate the logo beyond 45 degrees**
4. **Do not mirror or flip the logo**
5. **Maintain correct relationship between text and symbol**

### Brand Story

#### Origins:
Nuri began with a simple observation: Korean culture contains countless beautiful details - from the perfect curve of a calligraphy brush to the harmony of a Korean tea ceremony. These small, intentional moments of beauty create profound impact on daily life.

Our founder noticed how Western lifestyle products often prioritized function over form, and that Korean aesthetic wisdom was underappreciated globally. This inspired the creation of a brand that honors Korean cultural depth while serving contemporary global tastes.

#### Mission:
Nuri exists to bring "small things, quiet joy" into everyday life through Korean-inspired design. Every product is a homage to Korean cultural wisdom, reimagined for modern global consumers who appreciate cultural authenticity and refined aesthetics.

#### Values in Practice:
1. **Design Philosophy**: Every curve, color, and detail choice honors Korean aesthetic principles
2. **Material Selection**: Ethical sourcing with emphasis on traditional Korean materials
3. **Production Ethics**: Fair trade practices with Korean artisans
4. **Customer Experience**: Cultural education alongside product appreciation

## Performance & Accessibility

### Performance Requirements:
1. **Website Load**: Under 2.5 seconds on mobile, 3.0 seconds on desktop
2. **Image Optimization**: WebP format, responsive sizing, lazy loading
3. **Mobile-First**: Touch-friendly interactions, readable text at all sizes
4. **SEO**: Semantic HTML, structured data, meta descriptions
5. **Security**: HTTPS, security headers, regular security audits

### Accessibility Standards:
1. **WCAG 2.1 AA**: Full compliance for digital products
2. **Screen Reader Support**: Semantic HTML, ARIA labels
3. **Keyboard Navigation**: All functionality accessible without mouse
4. **Color Contrast**: Minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text
5. **Text Size**: Adjustable without breaking layout
6. **Content Structure**: Clear headings, logical information hierarchy

4. Ownership Statement

All original intellectual property developed by Shubham Chawhan for Nuri is owned by its creator unless and until it is formally assigned to a registered legal entity through a separate written agreement. Third-party assets remain subject to their respective licenses.

5. Third-Party Assets

The following assets are owned by their original creators or licensors and are used under their respective licenses:

  • Open Source Software, frameworks, and libraries
  • Fonts and icon sets
  • Licensed imagery and assets
  • External APIs (e.g. payment, mapping, email services)

6. Future Contributors

Future employees, interns, freelancers, contractors, designers, and developers should execute appropriate agreements regarding ownership of work created for Nuri before contributing.

7. Amendment Policy

This declaration may be updated by the founder through written documentation. Material changes (e.g. formal assignment to an entity) require a separate written agreement.

# Return & Refund Policy

## Our Promise to You

At Nuri, we want you to love every purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

## Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order at any time while it is still being processed. Orders can be cancelled when the status is `pending`, `confirmed`, or `processing`. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is no longer possible.

To cancel, email us at **nuri@shubhamos.com** with your order number and the word "Cancel." Cancellation is completely free.

## Returns & Refunds

We stand behind the quality of our products. If you receive a defective or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery at **nuri@shubhamos.com** with:

- Your order number
- A description of the issue
- Photos of the defect or incorrect item (if applicable)

We review each case individually and will work with you to find a fair resolution — whether that's a replacement, exchange, or refund.

## What We Need From You

- Items must be unused and in their original packaging (for defective/incorrect items).
- Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order.
- Provide clear documentation of the issue.

## Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Shipping Costs

- If the return is due to our error (defective or wrong item), we cover return shipping.
- If you change your mind about a purchase, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

## Non-Returnable Items

Certain items may not be eligible for return due to hygiene or customization reasons. These will be clearly marked on the product page.

## Need Help?

Reach out to us at **nuri@shubhamos.com** — we're happy to assist.

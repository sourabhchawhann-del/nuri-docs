# Consumer Rights & Legal Contact

## Your Rights as a Customer

When you shop with Nuri, you're entitled to:

- **Products as described** — Items must match their descriptions on our website.
- **Acceptable quality** — Products should be free from defects and fit for their intended purpose.
- **Fair pricing** — All prices are displayed in INR, with applicable taxes shown at checkout.
- **Returns and refunds** — Please see our Return & Refund Policy for details.

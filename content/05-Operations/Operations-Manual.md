## 1. Adding Products

### Objective
Standardized process for adding new products to the Nuri catalog while maintaining brand consistency and operational efficiency.

### Required Information
1. **Product Details**
   - English and Korean product names
   - Product description (benefits and features)
   - Technical specifications (dimensions, weight, materials)
   - Pricing information (MRP, selling price, currency)

2. **Product Classification**
   - Category (Stickers, Stationery, Accessories)
   - Subcategory (Art, Calligraphy, Pattern, etc.)
   - Collection identification
   - SKU code (following established naming convention)

3. **Visual Assets**
   - Product images (front, back, details, lifestyle)
   - Alt-text for accessibility
   - Brand-aligned photography style
   - Image specifications (300+ DPI, CMYK color space)

4. **Quality Information**
   - Material composition
   - Origin/manufacturing details
   - Quality certifications
   - Environmental impact assessment

### Approval Process

#### Step 1: Initial Review
- Submit product proposal through internal request form
- Product manager conducts initial feasibility assessment
- Brand compliance team reviews design and cultural authenticity

#### Step 2: Quality Assessment
- Quality control team verifies production capabilities
- Sample production and testing approval
- Supplier evaluation and negotiation

#### Step 3: Documentation Completion
- Complete product upload checklist
- Create comprehensive SEO metadata
- Establish inventory management parameters

#### Step 4: Final Approval
- Cross-departmental review (Product, Marketing, Operations, Finance)
- Sign-off by department heads
- Final integration into product database

### Timeline
- Draft submission: 1-2 days
- Internal review: 3-5 days
- Production testing: 5-10 days
- Final approval: 8-15 days total

## 2. Receiving Orders

### Standard Operating Procedure

#### Step 1: Order Receipt
1. Monitor customer order inputs through website
2. Verify payment processing through Razorpay
3. Create initial order record in inventory system
4. Generate unique order ID for tracking

#### Step 2: Order Validation
1. Verify inventory availability for all ordered items
2. Check customer information completeness
3. Validate shipping address and contact details
4. Process any applicable taxes and duties

#### Step 3: Order Acceptance
1. Send automated order confirmation to customer
2. Assign order to fulfillment team
3. Create packing list and staging area
4. Schedule inventory pick-up if necessary

### Quality Checks
- Product verification against order list
- Quality inspection for damaged or defective items
- Packaging integrity assessment
- Documentation completeness verification

## 3. Packaging Orders

### Packaging Standards

#### Primary Packaging Requirements:
1. **Eco-Friendly Materials**
   - Recycled kraft paper (minimum 80# brightness)
   - Soy-based inks for printing
   - Minimal plastic usage
   - Biodegradable tape

2. **Product Protection**
   - Individual product wrapping
   - Bubble wrap or foam for fragile items
   - Corrugated box with proper cushioning
   - Order-specific compartmentalization

3. **Brand Presentation**
   - Nuri watermark or branding
   - Consistent color scheme
   - Professional presentation
   - Return address information

#### Packaging Process:
1. **Assemble Packaging Materials**
   - Select appropriate box size
   - Pre-print return addresses
   - Prepare cushioning materials

2. **Product Placement**
   - Place items securely inside box
   - Use dividers for fragile items
   - Ensure stability during transit
   - Check for sharp edges or protrusions

3. **Documentation**
   - Insert packing slip
   - Include product care instructions
   - Add thank you note
   - Secure seal with professional tape

4. **Quality Assurance**
   - Visual inspection of packed box
   - Verify order completeness
   - Check sealing integrity
   - Confirm barcode/scanning readiness

## 4. Shipping Orders

### Shipping Procedure

#### Step 1: Order Preparation
1. Generate shipping label with tracking number
2. Print packing slip and invoice
3. Attach return label (if applicable)
4. Stage packages for courier pickup or drop-off

#### Step 2: Courier Selection
1. **Domestic Shipping (Within India)**
   - Standard: 2-5 business days
   - Express: 1-2 business days
   - Factors: Weight, destination, delivery speed

2. **International Shipping**
   - Standard: 7-21 business days
   - Express: 3-7 business days
   - Considerations: Customs, duties, tax

#### Step 3: Tracking and Notification
1. Update order status in inventory system
2. Generate tracking number
3. Send shipping confirmation to customer
4. Update website tracking information

#### Step 4: Customer Communication
1. **Shipping Confirmation**
   - Order number and details
   - Estimated delivery date
   - Tracking number and link
   - Packaging notes (if any)

2. **Delivery Updates**
   - Real-time status updates
   - Delivery delay notifications
   - Signature confirmation requests (for high-value items)

### Shipping Rate Guidelines:
1. **Free Shipping Threshold**: Orders over ₹1,499 (within India)
2. **Express Shipping**: 15-20% of order value
3. **International Shipping**: Calculated based on destination and weight
4. **Bulk Orders**: Custom pricing for orders over 100 items

## 5. Returns

### Return Policy Overview
- **Return Window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Condition**: Unused, undamaged, with original packaging
- **Refund Method**: Original payment method
- **Refund Processing**: 5-7 business days after return receipt

### Return Processing Procedure

#### Step 1: Return Request
1. Customer initiates return through website portal
2. Provide order number and reason for return
3. System generates return authorization
4. Customer packs item for return shipment

#### Step 2: Return Inspection
1. Receive returned item at warehouse
2. Quality inspection for damage or defects
3. Verify original packaging condition
4. Record return reason and outcome

#### Step 3: Refund Processing
1. Approve or reject return based on policy criteria
2. Process refund to original payment method
3. Send refund confirmation to customer
4. Update customer order status

#### Step 4: Inventory Update
1. Restock returned items (if resalable)
2. Update inventory levels in system
3. Investigate root cause (if defective)
4. Initiate quality improvement if necessary

### Return Authorization Codes:
- **RA-001**: Defective/Damaged Item
- **RA-002**: Wrong Item Shipped
- **RA-003**: Item Not as Described
- **RA-004**: Customer Change of Mind
- **RA-005**: Size/Fit Issue
- **RA-006**: Insufficient Quantity

## 7. Inventory Updates

### Inventory Management Process

#### Daily Operations
1. **Morning Reconciliation**
   - Check overnight sales reports
   - Verify inventory levels
   - Identify low stock items
   - Update safety stock levels

2. **Order Processing**
   - Receive new stock notifications
   - Update inventory after sales
   - Process backorders if necessary
   - Record adjustments for damaged items

3. **Quality Control**
   - Inspect incoming shipments
   - Quality check for all items
   - Record any defects or issues
   - Update inventory for rejects

#### Weekly Procedures
1. **Stock Analysis**
   - Review inventory turnover rates
   - Identify slow-moving items
   - Plan promotional activities
   - Calculate reorder points

2. **Supplier Coordination**
   - Review supplier performance
   - Place replenishment orders
   - Negotiate pricing terms
   - Schedule deliveries

3. **Warehouse Optimization**
   - Space utilization analysis
   - Storage strategy review
   - Picking efficiency assessment
   - Labor planning

### Inventory Control Systems

#### Reorder Point System
- **Formula**: Reorder Point = (Average Daily Usage × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
- **Safety Stock**: 3-5 days of sales
- **Maximum Stock**: 30 days of sales for high-demand items
- **Minimum Stock**: 10% of average daily usage

#### Inventory Valuation
- **FIFO**: First In, First Out
- **Weighted Average**: Average cost method
- **LIFO**: Last In, First Out (where tax-efficient)

### Inventory Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Target | Purpose |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| Stock Turnover Rate | Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory | 6-12 times/year | Efficiency |
| Days Inventory on Hand | (Average Inventory / Cost of Goods Sold) × 365 | 30 days | Liquidity |
| Fill Rate | Orders Fulfilled / Orders Requested | 98% | Service Level |
| Stockout Frequency | Number of Stockouts / Total Days | < 0.5% | Availability |

## 10. Emergency Recovery

### Disaster Recovery Procedures

#### Step 1: Immediate Response (0-2 hours)
1. **Incident Detection**
   - Monitor system alerts
   - Identify affected systems
   - Assess severity and scope
   - Notify relevant teams

2. **Containment Actions**
   - Isolate affected systems
   - Block malicious activity
   - Protect customer data
   - Preserve evidence

#### Step 2: Short-term Recovery (2-24 hours)
1. **Assessment and Planning**
   - Document incident details
   - Identify recovery resources
   - Develop recovery plan
   - Estimate impact timeframe

2. **Recovery Execution**
   - Restore from recent backup
   - Apply system updates
   - Verify system stability
   - Test restored functionality

#### Step 3: Long-term Recovery (24 hours - 1 week)
1. **Root Cause Analysis**
   - Identify failure points
   - Implement preventive measures
   - Update recovery procedures
   - Train staff on new processes

2. **System Improvements**
   - Patch vulnerabilities
   - Update security protocols
   - Improve monitoring systems
   - Enhance disaster recovery plans

### Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)

| System | Recovery Time | Backup Frequency |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Database | 2-4 hours | Daily backups |
| Website | 1-2 hours | Multiple daily |
| Applications | 30 minutes | Hourly backups |
| Email Systems | 2 hours | Daily backups |
